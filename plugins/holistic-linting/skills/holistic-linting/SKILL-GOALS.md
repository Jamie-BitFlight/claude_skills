The purpose and explicit goals of the skill holistic-linting:

1. Run the quality gates applicable to changed work and produce reliable evidence of their results.
2. Turn quality-gate failures into evidence-backed corrections of their underlying cause rather than merely hiding the reported symptom.
3. Route diagnosis and correction to the appropriate domain capability without duplicating language-specific engineering knowledge.
4. Preserve product behavior, repository policy, and quality-gate integrity while resolving diagnostics.
5. Expose unresolved and out-of-scope diagnostics without silently discarding them.
