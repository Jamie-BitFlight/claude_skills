# Prose Style Reference

Source basis: `hardikpandya/stop-slop` (`SKILL.md`, `references/phrases.md`,
`references/structures.md`, accessed 2026-06-08). This preserves the source-specific distinctions that are not
already stated in the [writing contract](writing-contract.md).

## Retained distinctions

- Put the reader in the scene when appropriate; use direct language instead of a detached lecturer voice.
- Avoid false agency from inanimate nouns when a person or team can be named.
- Keep rhythm uneven: avoid repetitive three-beat patterns and em dashes.
- Trust the reader: remove performative sincerity and explanations of what the text is about to do.
- When a modifier carries the sentence, rewrite the sentence rather than relying on the modifier.
- Write to be understood, not engineered to be quoted.

## Phrase and structure scan

- Remove announcement openers such as "here's why" and "the truth is".
- Remove empty emphasis such as "full stop" and "let that sink in".
- Replace broad hedges and lazy extremes doing vague work with the actual claim.
- Avoid negation-then-reveal structures and lists of what something is not before saying what it is.
- Avoid fragment stacks used for drama, rhetorical questions answered immediately, and paragraph endings that all land
  as punch lines.
- Prefer a concrete subject to a wh-word sentence opening when one can lead.

## Return-time check

Before returning style-edited prose, verify that it has no em dashes, repetitive three-beat patterns, false agency
from inanimate subjects, performative sincerity, detached lecturer voice, announcement opener, empty emphasis,
fragment stack, immediately answered rhetorical question, or repeated punch-line ending.
