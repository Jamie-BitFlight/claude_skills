# Skill Content Optimization

Before creating or editing any skill file, load the `/plugin-creator:skill-creator` skill — see
root `AGENTS.md`'s "Load `/plugin-creator:skill-creator`..." bullet for the full trigger
conditions, and (if the skill belongs to a plugin) `plugin-development.md`'s Prerequisite Skills
section for additional schema-reference skills. `skill-creator` is the authoritative source for
skill structure, frontmatter schema, progressive disclosure patterns, and content quality
standards — including imperative language, avoiding decorative `**bold**`, and structured
XML-tag sections.
